## Philosophy 

I love doing public outreach events.  The primary events that I volunteer for are designed for children looking to find a way into science.  The energy and excitement they have for learning is so inspiring.

## Events

I volunteer at the annual events 

* Science Rendezvous (Victoria)
* UVic ATLAS Masterclass 

As an undergraduate, I also volunteered at the UVic Astronomy Open House running the Q&A station.
